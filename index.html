The Studio

Design With
Intention.

Lumen Interior Studio is a Brooklyn-based design practice focused on residential work. We approach each project as a collaboration — listening first, then translating the way you actually live into a considered spatial and material language.

Our work is quiet. We favour honest materials, natural light, and proportions that feel right to the body. The goal is never decoration for its own sake, but interiors that feel inevitable — as though they could not have been arranged any other way.

Whether it is a single room or an entire home, we treat the process with the same care: measured, personal, and unhurried.

Philosophy

Less Noise.
More Meaning.

We believe good interiors are not about how much is added, but about what is left out. Proportion, light, and material carry more than decoration ever could.

Natural materials age honestly. Light changes across the day. Function should never be sacrificed for looks. These are the principles that guide our work.
